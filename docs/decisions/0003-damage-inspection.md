# 3. Damage inspection: nothing confirmed by a model, and the faster vision model first

An inspect mission sends photos to a vision model and keeps what it reports for a person to review.

- **A finding is a question for a person.** Every finding starts pending, whatever the model's confidence. Only `FindingStore.set_status` changes that, and no code path reaches it from a model. A person's dismissal also survives later looks at the same spot.
- **The model's words are data.** A photo can hold text and a model can repeat it. `agent/vision.py` lets out a bool, a cleaned sentence capped at 240 characters, one of four severities and a number. The sentence stays out of event text, which the agent reads, and travels in the event's data.
- **Ask for reports, not verdicts.** The first prompt listed what isn't damage, and both models then explained real scorch marks away as shadows or printed marks (14 and 13 of 20 right). Telling them a person checks every report and a miss costs more than a false alarm, and giving reasoning room to finish, raised Nano to 17 of 20. Super stayed at 14. It also means a cable or a black mat sometimes gets reported.
- **Nano before Super.** On 60 simulated photos (three drawings of the same 20 scenes, two of them not used for tuning the prompt), Nano Omni got 51 right with 3 misses and 6 false alarms at about 3 s an image. Super VL got 45 right with 12 misses and 3 false alarms at about 7 s. A miss is the costly error, and three drones can produce a photo every 1.3 s at worst. `NEMOTRON_VISION_MODEL` puts a different model first. Neither has been scored on real video, which could reverse this. Save labelled frames in a folder with a `labels.json` and run `python -m swarm.inspect_eval --folder DIR`.
- **One photo per view.** Each drone's sharpest frame per second is considered, and one from within 0.35 m and 25 degrees of a view already sent adds nothing. Vision runs on three workers behind a queue of 16 that drops the oldest, because the flight loop must never wait. A dropped photo's view can be sent again, and the operator is told when this starts.
